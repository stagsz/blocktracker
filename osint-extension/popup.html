What is OSINT?

OSINT stands for Open Source Intelligence, the practice of collecting and analyzing publicly available information from open sources to produce actionable intelligence. Open sources include social media platforms, search engines, public records, news media, websites, government data, academic publications, and geospatial information. This information is legally accessible without requiring hacking, breaking in, or special authorization.

OSINT is used across corporate competitive intelligence, security threat assessment, investigative journalism, and research. Unlike other intelligence types such as HUMINT (human sources) or SIGINT (intercepted communications), OSINT focuses exclusively on publicly available information that can be legally and ethically accessed.

This tool generates AI prompts that help analysts systematically conduct OSINT investigations using a proven 4-phase methodology: Discovery, Signals, Insights, and Receipts. Each generated prompt guides the analysis process from initial target identification through evidence documentation, ensuring thorough and professional intelligence reports.
